About

Marketing, with the receipts

My biggest strength is combining my creativity with strategic planning. I enjoy planning campaigns and then getting my hands dirty creating content.

For the last 4.5 years I ran growth at Speero, an experimentation and CRO agency, reporting directly to the CEO. I owned the newsletter, LinkedIn, blog, paid, and SEO, while writing, designing, strategizing, experimenting, and reporting on results.

I also build complex AI workflows that write blog posts and audit them for SEO/AEO based on AI citation readiness, content quality, E-E-A-T signals, technical SEO, and other SEO elements.

My other skills and agents include ad audits, SVG designers, case study and LinkedIn (ghost)writers, plus a DIY AEO tracking app.

My no-slop skill catches 70+ tiered AI-writing patterns, from binary contrasts, colon reveals and em dashes to invented stats, weasel attributions and fake-profound closers, plus the rhythm tells, empty hedging and banned buzzwords that give a draft away.
